Live now

Already in the app. Most of this landed in November 2026.

  • New

    Easier for everyone to use

    GFD works with your phone's text size, screen reader and calmer-motion setting.

    • Bigger buttons, at least a fingertip wide
    • Stronger text contrast in every colour theme, light and dark
    • The rest timer and messages are read aloud with TalkBack
  • New

    Quicker number entry

    Change weight and reps with one thumb, mid-set.

    • Hold a number box and slide up or down to change it
    • Tap it for the big GFD keypad, with Next to jump to reps and the next set
    • Tap a filled-in value to replace it in one go
  • New

    Free and Pro

    Every new account gets 14 days of Pro, no card needed.

    • Free keeps your logs, history and up to 3 plans, for good
    • Pro adds unlimited plans, full stats and every measurement
    • Going back to Free never deletes anything you made
  • New

    Your privacy, your choice

    Plain-English privacy policy and terms, readable before you sign up.

    • See what you agreed to and when, in Profile › Privacy
    • Tips and news emails only if you tick the box, and off again any time
    • Injury notes are kept only with your permission, and deleted everywhere if you change your mind
    • GFD has its own home at getfitdaily.co.za
  • New

    Plan and review on a bigger screen

    Open GFD on a tablet or computer and it spreads out.

    • Plan your week on one screen: drag exercises between days and change sets and reps in place
    • Your charts side by side, any dates you like, and up to 3 lifts compared on one chart (Pro)
    • Every workout in a table you can filter, with several deleted at once
  • New

    Your data, yours to take or delete

    In More, Profile, under Your data and account.

    • Download everything GFD keeps about you, with your sets and measurements as spreadsheet files
    • Delete your account and everything in it, with 7 days to change your mind
    • No app? The same on the Delete your account page
  • New

    Kilograms or pounds

    Log in the unit on your plates. Switch any time in More.

    • Sets, last time, personal bests, volume and body weight all show in your unit
    • Switching never changes what you lifted: 100 kg stays 100 kg, shown as 220.5 lb
    • Your choice follows you to every phone you sign in on
  • New

    The right exercise for your gear

    Smarter swaps when a machine is busy or you're training somewhere else.

    • Swap any exercise mid-workout: the same movement comes first, with a short reason why
    • Swap for today only, or change your plan for good
    • A "Machine taken" button for a one-tap swap, just for today
    • Bodyweight and floor exercises always available, wherever you train
    • Studio pilates moves offer a mat or band version when you're away
    • Replace an exercise straight from Edit workout
  • New

    A start that fits who you are

    Setup asks about you before it hands you a plan.

    • Four quick questions: what you train, your experience, your goal and how many days a week
    • A suggested plan that suits your answers and the gear where you train
    • New starter plans: beginner full body, mat pilates, yoga and running
    • Setup picks up where you left off if you close the app
    • Change your answers any time in Profile
  • New

    Fix any set you logged

    Typed 850 instead of 85? Put it right, and everything that depends on it follows.

    • Edit or delete any set, or a whole workout, from History
    • Personal bests, "last time" and charts rebuild from what you actually lifted
    • A friendly check before saving a jump that looks like a typo
    • Each set starts from last session's real numbers, so what you see is what you save
  • New

    Personal bests that mean something

    Records that fit the exercise, and celebrations you've earned.

    • Best weight for 1, 3, 5, 8 and 10 reps, plus an estimated one-rep max
    • Most reps for bodyweight moves, longest holds, most rounds, longest or fastest cardio
    • No confetti for a set where the planned reps weren't done
    • "Last time" matches the same plan day and rep target
    • Pick your celebration style: Energetic or Calm
  • New

    Guided body check-in

    Measure yourself in under a minute, one swipe card at a time.

    • Choose what you track, including left and right sides
    • A history and chart for every measurement, with goals and reminders
  • New

    Flow, Drift and Glow colour themes

    Three softer looks next to the classic volt green, each in light and dark.

  • New

    Works offline

    Add GFD to your home screen and keep logging when the gym Wi-Fi gives up. Your sets sync when you're back online.

  • Since day one

    The foundations

    Everything you need to plan and log a training week.

    • More than 1,000 exercises with step-by-step safety tips
    • More than 400 pieces of gear, so plans only use what your gym has
    • Illustrated exercise guides, with more added in every batch
    • Ready-made plans, your own plans, rest timer and Stats

Up next

The features we're working on now.

  • Building next

    Plain words and calmer screens

    Every screen easy to read on your first day.

    • Clearer names, and tap to explain terms like "3 × 10–12" or volume
    • No buttons hiding under the timer or the tab bar
    • Your own plans listed first
  • Building next

    GFD on Google Play

    The Android app is built and getting ready for the store.

    • Rest timer alerts, even with your phone locked
    • Works offline from the first time you open it
    • Your account, plans and history, the same as on the web

Coming soon

Easier sign-in, and Pro wherever you are.

  • Planned

    Easier sign-in and Pro anywhere

    Fewer passwords, and one Pro for phone and web.

    • Sign in with Google, and a show-password button
    • Buy Pro in the Android app or on the website, and use it on both

Train smarter

GFD starts telling you what to do next.

  • Planned

    Log it the way you train

    Room for the details serious training needs.

    • Rest time per exercise, with sensible defaults
    • Warm-up, top and back-off sets, and how hard a set felt (RPE)
    • Left and right for one-sided moves
    • Session notes, plus setup notes like seat height or springs
  • Planned

    Your progression coach

    The app works out your next weight, so you don't have to.

    • "Next time: 10 kg" after every workout, based on how it went
    • Holds after a tough day, and a lighter week when you need one
    • "How did that feel?" to help beginners find the right weight
    • Week-by-week programmes, from a 4-week starter to 5×5 and beyond
    • Plate calculator and automatic warm-up sets
  • Planned

    Stats that tell the truth

    Numbers you can trust, led by what matters to you.

    • A headline that fits your training: weight lifted, minutes or sessions
    • A weekly goal you set yourself
    • Progress charts per exercise, and a strength dashboard for the big lifts
  • Planned

    Your training week

    GFD knows which day it is in your plan.

    • A week strip with ticks for the days you've done
    • Missed a day? "Do Thursday's session today?"
    • "I have 20 minutes" trims today's workout to what matters most

Polish and depth

Making the everyday parts faster and richer.

  • Planned

    More body progress

    • Progress photos with a side-by-side slider
    • Check-ins for flexibility, balance and posture, like sit-and-reach
  • Planned

    Gear setup in minutes

    • One-tap setups: commercial gym, home rack, pilates studio and more
    • Add a whole station, like bench, bar and plates, in one go
    • Pick gear by picture, and a "What's this machine?" guide
  • Planned

    Find and learn any exercise

    • One search everywhere, with filters for discipline, level and your gear
    • Add to today or to your plan straight from the library
    • Your own history and bests on every exercise page
    • Pictures first, so you see the move before your first set

Further out

Bigger ideas that grow GFD beyond the gym floor.

  • Planned

    Pilates mode

    • Flow sessions: one tick per move, no sets and no rest timer
    • Remember spring settings and footbar for every reformer move
    • Full classical mat order, reformer, Cadillac and chair moves
    • A teaching view with the current and next move
  • Planned

    For coaches and classes

    • Build a programme once and give it to your clients
    • See their logs, missed sessions and comments in one place
    • Run class sequences and share home programmes by link
  • Planned

    Health, food and friends

    • Connect with health apps
    • A simple daily protein and calorie target
    • Train with a gym buddy and see each other's check-ins
    • Bring your history from other workout apps

And then?

You tell us. Many of the features above came straight from people training with GFD. Send your ideas from More → Suggestions in the app.

This roadmap shows the order we plan to build in. Plans can change as we learn what helps most, so there are no fixed dates.
