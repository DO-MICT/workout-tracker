Privacy policy

Version 2026.11.08 · in effect from 8 November 2026

GFD helps you plan, log and track your training. To do that it keeps your workouts and body data. This page says what we keep, why, who helps us run it, and how you stay in control. Short version: your data is yours, we don't sell it, there are no ads, and you can download or delete it at any time.

Who we are

GFD is run by Gideon Oosthuizen, [postal address], South Africa ("we", "us"). We decide how your personal information is used, so we are the controller (GDPR) and the responsible party (POPIA).

Questions, requests or complaints: privacy@getfitdaily.co.za. Our information officer is Gideon Oosthuizen. We reply within 30 days, usually much sooner.

Who helps us run GFD

These providers process data for us under contracts that limit them to doing so on our instructions:

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and file storage. Your account and training data are stored in the European Union (Paris, France).
  • GitHub (Microsoft): hosts the website and web app. Like any web host it sees your IP address when pages load.
  • Google Fonts: the app's lettering is loaded from Google, which sees your IP address when it does.
  • Email sender: delivers sign-in, confirmation and password emails, and tips and news if you ask for them.
  • Google Play, RevenueCat and Paddle (our web payment partner and merchant of record): handle Pro purchases, receipts and refunds. Paddle and Google act as sellers in their own right for payments and have their own privacy policies.

Some of these providers are based in, or may access data from, countries outside the EU, UK or South Africa, such as the United States. Where that happens the transfer is protected by safeguards recognised by those laws, such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or an adequacy decision.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and training data: until you delete your account.
  • When you ask to delete your account you have 7 days to change your mind (sign in and tap Keep my account). After that, everything linked to it is removed, at the latest within 30 days of your request. Copies in our provider's backups roll off within its backup window, at most 30 days later.
  • Purchase records: as long as tax law requires, usually 5 to 7 years, kept by the payment provider.
  • Usage counts 400 days, error reports 90 days, the trial check code for as long as GFD offers trials.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can:

  • See and download your data: Profile, Download my data gives you a ZIP with everything we keep about you, including your sets and measurements as spreadsheets. Or email us and we'll send a copy.
  • Correct it, in the app or by asking us.
  • Delete your account and everything in it, in the app (Profile, Delete my account), on the delete your account page, or by emailing privacy@getfitdaily.co.za from the address on your account.
  • Change your mind about anything you agreed to by choice, such as tips and news or health details, in More, Profile, Privacy. That doesn't affect what we did before.
  • Object to or ask us to limit how we use your data, and ask us to move it to another service.
  • Complain to a regulator: in South Africa the Information Regulator (inforegulator.org.za); in the EU your local data protection authority; in the UK the ICO (ico.org.uk). We'd like the chance to help first.

California and other US states: you have the right to know, access, correct and delete your personal information, and to not be treated differently for using these rights. We don't sell or share personal information as those laws define it, and we don't use sensitive information to infer things about you.

We may ask you to confirm a request from the email address on your account, so nobody else can get your data.

Keeping it safe

Data travels encrypted (HTTPS) and is stored encrypted by our providers. Each person can only reach their own records: the database itself checks this on every request, and we test it automatically before every change. Photos are private and shown through short-lived links. Only a small number of admins can read suggestions sent to us; nobody at GFD browses your workouts. If a breach ever puts your data at risk, we'll tell you and the regulators as the law requires.

Children

GFD is for people aged 16 and over. We don't knowingly keep data about anyone younger. If you think a child has signed up, email privacy@getfitdaily.co.za and we'll delete the account.

Changes to this policy

When we change this policy we give it a new version number. If the change matters, the app shows it to you and asks you to agree before you carry on. Earlier versions are available on request.
