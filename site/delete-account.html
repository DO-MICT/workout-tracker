Delete your account

You can delete your GFD account and all your data at any time, in the app or on this page. It's free and you don't need to give a reason.

In the app

Open GFD, tap More, then Profile, scroll to Your data and account and tap Delete my account. Type DELETE to confirm.

On this page

Sign in with the email and password of the account you want to delete.

Type DELETE to confirm that you want your account and everything in it deleted.

Changed your mind? Tap Keep my account (here or in the app) before then.

Your account is kept. Nothing will be deleted.

What happens

  • You have 7 days to change your mind. Sign in again (in the app or here) and tap Keep my account.
  • After 7 days we delete your account and everything in it: plans, workouts, sets, records, measurements, notes, gear, settings, consent records, feedback and screenshots you sent, and your link to any Pro purchase.
  • What stays: a one-way scrambled code made from your email (not the address itself) so the free trial is once per person, and usage counts that never had your name, email or account id. Purchase records are kept by Google Play or Paddle as tax law requires.
  • Copies in our provider's backups roll off within 30 days.

Paying for Pro?

Deleting your account doesn't cancel a subscription. Cancel it first: on Android open Google Play, then Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions, GFD, Cancel. If you bought on the web, use the link in your receipt email.

Want a copy first?

In the app, tap Profile, then Download my data for a ZIP of everything we keep about you. Or email us and we'll send it.
